# Zap of Nakamoto — Design

Status: **draft, round 11** · Last updated 2026-10-26

Brand and character design for **Zap of Nakamoto**, a mobile wallet for people who use
bitcoin for the first time. The app in this repository applies it through White Wallet's design API:

- the palette and its colour roles: `res/values/zap_colors.xml`, `res/values*/brand_colors.xml`;
- the fonts: `res/font/` (licences in `fonts/`);
- the mark as Android drawables: `res/drawable*/`, written by `dev/mark.py` (a port of `assets/generate.py`);
- the living mark, its states and motion, the celebrations and the wordmark: `code/`;
- the voice and the slogan: `res/values*/strings.xml`.

## Product

- **What it does:** sends and receives small amounts of bitcoin instantly over Lightning (LNURL).
- **Who it's for:** beginners. They want to pay and get paid. They don't want to learn a wallet.
- **What it's not:** configurable, technical, or a wallet that does everything.
- **Design values:** easy, minimalistic, beautiful, simple by design.

## Brand principles

1. **Zap is the focus.** The name, the mark and the motion are all built around one zap.
2. **Safe and calm.** A first-timer should feel looked after, not excited or rushed.
3. **Bold and graphic.** Use flat shapes, strong contrast, a single accent colour and no gradients.
4. **Friendly, not cute.** The character shows how things are going with its face, but it
   doesn't perform. No hops, winks or nods.
5. **Words over jargon.** Say "bitcoin", never "BTC" or "sats". The custodian is
   "a Lightning service".

## The mark: Zap

A chubby lightning bolt with a face. The face is cut out of the bolt, so it shows
whatever is behind it and works on any background.

| | |
|---|---|
| Bolt path | `M62 6L16 58H44L34 94L84 38H57L70 6Z` |
| Bolt stroke | 12, `stroke-linejoin: round`, same colour as the fill (this makes it chubby) |
| ViewBox | `-10 -10 120 120` |
| Eyes | Dots, r 3.8, at (41, 47) and (58, 45) |
| Mouth | Quadratic curve `M x0 y0 Q cx cy x1 y1`, stroke 3.6, round caps |
| Face | An SVG mask cut-out (black in the mask means a hole in the bolt) |

Files: [`assets/mark-ink.svg`](assets/mark-ink.svg), [`assets/mark-spark.svg`](assets/mark-spark.svg),
[`assets/app-icon.svg`](assets/app-icon.svg) (ink on spark, 512). All SVGs are generated by
[`assets/generate.py`](assets/generate.py), so edit the script, not the files.

**Rules**
- Use one colour per mark: ink on light or spark backgrounds, spark on ink.
- Never draw the face in a second colour; it is always a cut-out.
- Never outline, add a gradient or add a drop shadow.
- Proposed, not yet tested: the minimum size is 24 px. Below that, use the bolt without the face.

## Wordmark

The display face is **Unbounded**. "zap" is set in lowercase and large. "of Nakamoto" is set
small underneath it, or beside it on wide layouts. Round 7 explored geometric custom
lettering; it was dropped.

## States

The mark is the app's status indicator. Each state is an eye and mouth pair. The mouth
morphs between its 6 numbers, so every transition is smooth.

| State | When | Eyes | Mouth `[x0,y0,cx,cy,x1,y1]` | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rest** | Has a balance, idle | dots | smile `44,55.5,51,61.5,58,53.5` | Floats and glances aside while idle |
| **Curious** | Empty wallet (₿0) | dots | small `47.5,56.5,51,59,54.5,56` | Head tilt −9° |
| **Waiting** | Receive screen open | dots looking up-right (+2, −1.5) | focus `46,56,51,58.5,56,55.5` | Slow breathing, scale 1.04, 1.4 s |
| **Focus** | Payment going out | dots | focus | — |
| **Working** | The loading screen: the wallet updates, checks the zaps or looks after them | dots | focus | The ring pulses, the bolt bobs |
| **Happy** | Payment arrived | arcs `M36.5 48.5Q41 43 45.5 48.5` and `M53.5 46.5Q58 41 62.5 46.5` | big `42.5,54,51,66,60,52` | — |
| **Oops** | Not enough funds, no connection | dots | worry `45,59,51,54,57,58` | Small shake |

Idle blink: the open dots become short lines for 130 ms, about every 3 s; one blink in five is a double, the
second 120 ms after the first.

Idle glance: at Rest, every 10–20 s, the dots look to one side (±2.5, +0.5) over 280 ms, stay 0.9–1.4 s and come
back over 320 ms.

Files: [`assets/states/`](assets/states).

## Motion

Two rules: **receive** comes down, and **send** is receive played backwards. Motion is
limited to the bolt's position, squash and one flash ring. Nothing gets added on top.

**Receive** (650 ms + 220 ms face)
1. The bolt drops in from above: `translateY(-70%)`, opacity 0 → squash `translateY(6%) scale(1.08, .9)` at 60 % → rest.
   Easing `cubic-bezier(.3, 1.4, .5, 1)`.
2. A flash ring (r 50, stroke 3) goes out: scale .7 → 1.35, opacity .8 → 0, 700 ms ease-out.
3. The face becomes Happy over 220 ms.

**Send** (about 1.7 s)
1. The face becomes Focus over 200 ms.
2. The flash ring pulls in: scale 1.35 → .75 (opacity .8) → .7 (opacity 0), 480 ms ease-in.
3. After 260 ms the bolt squashes and rises out of the top: rest → squash at 35 % → `translateY(-70%)`, opacity 0.
   650 ms, `cubic-bezier(.5, 0, .7, .4)`.
4. After a 220 ms pause it fades back in place: scale .94 → 1, 420 ms ease-out. The face settles to Rest.
   Send never ends on Happy, so sending stays calmer than receiving.

**Oops:** the face becomes Worry, then shakes on the x axis (−6 %, 6 %, −4 %, 0) over 420 ms.

**Empty:** the head tilts −9° over 400 ms and the face shows the small smile. On the wallet the mark grows from
88 to 128 dp over the same 400 ms, above "Nothing here yet. Share your address to get your first zap." under the
balance; it shrinks back once money arrives.

**Working** (while the loading screen is up): the face becomes Focus over 200 ms. Then, again and again, the
receive's flash ring goes out (scale .7 → 1.35, opacity .8 → 0, 1.2 s ease-out) while the bolt bobs up 4 % and back
with it. It stops when the work is done and the balance shows again.

**Idle:** while nothing happens, the bolt floats up 3 % and back down, 4 s a round, ease-in-out sine. It eases into
and out of the float, so it never jumps when something else starts.

**Reduced motion:** all movement is skipped and only the face changes, instantly.

Implementation: Web Animations API on nested groups `.tilt > .mv` with
`transform-box: fill-box`. The mouth is tweened with `requestAnimationFrame` using ease-out cubic.
See [`prototype/zap-states.html`](prototype/zap-states.html).

## The zap

A zap is bitcoin as a QR code or link: whoever claims it gets the bitcoin (White Wallet calls it a note). Zap draws
it as a **spark card** (rounds 12 and 13), in [`code/`](../code) (`ZapNote.kt`, White Wallet's `NoteFace`).

**The card** is spark (`primaryContainer`) with 24 dp corners, at least 220 dp wide, with 16 × 14 dp padding and
10 dp between its parts. From the top: the state line, the mark (40 dp, ink), the amount (Unbounded,
`headlineLarge`), then "Scan to claim" above the QR code. The code stays exactly as White Wallet draws it, black on
white. The state line is in small capitals with 0.1 em letter spacing, with a dot while the zap is money.

| Where | State line | Look |
|---|---|---|
| A zap's page, waiting | "Waiting to be claimed" | Spark card; mark Waiting; the code under the cover until a tap |
| A zap's page, claimed | "Claimed" | Dashed 2 dp outline, the amount at 55 %; mark Happy |
| A zap's page, taken back | "Back in your wallet", on a spark pill | Solid 2 dp outline; mark Rest |
| Claim | "Ready to claim" | Spark card without a code; mark Happy |
| A zap's details (Zaps tab) | — | Spark card without a code; mark Rest |
| Paper | — | 2.5 dp ink outline on white, no fill, to save ink; mark Rest; "Scan to claim" |

**One mark.** On a zap's page the mark is on the card; the page has none of its own above it.

**The cover.** White Wallet covers the code until the user taps it, and again whenever the app leaves the screen.
Zap's cover is gold (`tertiary`) with 12 dp corners and a dashed edge in ink at 35 %, with "Tap to show the QR code"
in ink (`titleSmall`), so it reads as part of the card, not a hole in it. Paper is never covered.

**Motion.** A zap claimed while its page is open: the card's mark plays **Send** and ends Happy; 700 ms in, the
spark fill fades out over 300 ms, so the card empties once the mark has left it. A zap taken back changes at once.
A zap opened later shows its state settled, without motion. With reduced motion nothing moves.

**The Zaps tab** (White Wallet's notes view). Each row starts with a 32 dp spark tile, 8 dp corners, holding the bolt
without its face (18 dp, ink; faceless only below 24 dp), then the amount (`titleMedium`), what is unusual about the
zap (with its icon), and its age. While a zap is being paid or its payment is unclear, the tile is a dashed outline
and the bolt is muted. Handed-out zaps sit under their own heading on a turned-over tile, ink with a spark bolt
(raised ink in dark), with "Waiting to be claimed" and a dot under the amount and their mint below it. Above the total the mark
(56 dp) waits while zaps are out and rests otherwise; an empty tab shows it once, 112 dp and Curious, above
"No zaps yet." The mint headers are White Wallet's.

**Words.** The card's lines are Zap's (`code/src/main/res`): "Waiting to be claimed", "Scan to claim", "Claimed",
"Back in your wallet", "Ready to claim" (German "Wartet aufs Einlösen", "Zum Einlösen scannen", "Eingelöst",
"Zurück in deiner Wallet", "Bereit zum Einlösen"). A zap is claimed, never redeemed, and what it carries is bitcoin.

## Voice

- Short and warm, written in second person. "Waiting for a zap…", "Your first bitcoin!", "Zapped!"
- Amounts are shown as `₿1,000`.
- **Slogan:** **"Just zap it."** (German: "Einfach zappen.")
