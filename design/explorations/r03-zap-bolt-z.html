4 · Spark, worked out

The palette and where it goes in the app

Yellow is loud, so it carries the brand on big surfaces (icon, main button, the zap flash). Ink does the reading. One catch: white text on yellow is unreadable, so text on yellow is always ink.

The app's main colour is the one it uses for text and links too, and yellow text on a light screen can't be read. So in light mode the buttons are ink with yellow text, and yellow lives on the icon and the zap moment. In dark mode they swap and yellow leads. A first proposal, to check on a real phone.
