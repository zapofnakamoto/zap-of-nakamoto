Zap of Nakamoto · Round 4

Three new ideas, each telling a different story

Rounds 2 and 3 only refined a letter. These three start over from what the app means to a first-timer. Each keeps what you liked about D: a solid shape with a forward lean and some energy. Colour and type stay as chosen.

Round 3 favourite (D) for comparison. A strong letter, but it only says “Z”.

Concept 1 · Zap MessageSending bitcoin feels like sending a text

A chat bubble with a bolt cut out of it. Everyone already knows how to send a message, and that's the whole promise: if you can text, you can zap.

Strong becauseThe first-timer understands it right away. No bitcoin knowledge needed.
Watch outChat bubbles are common, so it needs the bolt and the lean to be ours.
Leo zapped you₿1,000 · “for the coffee ☕”
If you can text, you can zap.

Concept 2 · BetweenThe zap is what jumps between two people

Two dots for two people, and a bolt jumping from one to the other. The brand stops being about bitcoin and becomes about the moment you pay a friend back.

Strong becauseIt's the most abstract and the most ownable. The two dots can stand alone as a pattern in the app and in ads.
Watch outNeeds a sentence the first time to explain. It's also the thinnest at very small sizes.
Zapped to Mia₿2,500 arrived on her phone
From you to them. In a zap.

Concept 3 · ZappyA friendly guide for your first bitcoin

A chunky bolt with a face. Beginners are nervous about money apps. A character makes the app feel like a friend showing you around, the way Duolingo's owl does for learning.

Strong becauseBest for marketing: stickers, social posts, reactions. It can show feelings the app needs (success, waiting, empty).
Watch outThe most playful. If it shows up everywhere, money can feel like a toy.
