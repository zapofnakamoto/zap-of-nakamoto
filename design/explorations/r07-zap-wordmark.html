Zap of Nakamoto · Round 7 · The word is the logo

Just the word zap, drawn with care

No symbol and no character. The three letters are drawn from one stroke weight and one round bowl, and each variant adds one idea to them. “of Nakamoto” gets its own spot so it belongs to the logo when it's there and isn't missed when it's not.

2 · of Nakamoto

Where the rest of the name sits

The p's tail leaves a space under the word. Two lockups put “of Nakamoto” there, so it fits the word's shape instead of trailing behind it.

3 · The slogan

Six lines, from fast to reassuring

“Bitcoin, as easy as a zap.” works, but it's long and explains itself. Tap a line to try it on the poster and the store listing below. My pick: Bitcoin in a zap. as the slogan, with Bitcoin for your pocket. as the line that says “small amounts”.
