Zap of Nakamoto · round 12 · 2026-10-29

The zap you hand over

A zap is bitcoin as a QR code or link: whoever claims it gets the money. White Wallet now lets a brand draw it (NoteFace). Zap still uses the default, a large amount above the QR code. These are three directions for Zap's own.

  • WhereThe zap's page (waiting, claimed, taken back), the claim preview, and the printed page.
  • App keepsThe QR code exactly as drawn, the bearer warning, Share, Copy, Print, Take back.
  • Brand rulesFlat, one accent (spark), no gradients or shadows. Unbounded for the amount. Friendly, not cute.
  • On paperAlways the light theme on white, so ink-light versions are shown.
Direction A

Spark card

The zap is a spark card: the mark on top, the amount, the QR code on its own white margin. It's the brand's strongest moment. Once claimed or taken back, only the outline stays.

What doesn't change

  • The QR codeThe app draws it black on white. Every direction puts it on its own white margin and never tints, crops or covers it.
  • The mark aboveThe zap's page already shows the living mark above the zap (round 11 motion). A and B repeat a small, still mark on the zap; C replaces it with the faceless bolt.
  • The words around itThe bearer warning, "Not claimed yet…" and the buttons stay the app's, below the zap. The QR code here is a placeholder, not a real zap.
