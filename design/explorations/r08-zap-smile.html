Zap of Nakamoto · Round 8 · Fresh start

A zap that smiles

From Zappy we keep only the part that worked: a chunky bolt and a smile. No eyes and no character, so it reads as a logo, not a mascot. The logo is also the app's state light. It fills up when bitcoin arrives, leaps away when you send, and goes quiet when the wallet is empty.

zapof Nakamoto
1 · How it works

Three dials, nothing else moves

The shape never changes, so it stays recognisable. Every state is a mix of the same three dials.

Charge

An empty wallet is an outline. Bitcoin coming in fills the bolt from the bottom, like charging a battery.

Smile

One curve does all the feeling: resting, happy, focused, sorry. Small changes, easy to read.

Motion

Three moves only: it crouches and leaps (send), drops in with a flash (receive), and breathes (waiting).
