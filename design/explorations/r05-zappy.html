3 · Personality

Who Zappy is

Quick, warm, a little cheeky

Like the friend who sets up your phone for you. Helpful, a bit playful, never smug about knowing more.

Says little

  • Hi! Let's get you your first bitcoin.
  • Zapped! +₿1,000 from Leo
  • Nothing here yet. Share your address and I'll wait.
  • No signal. I'll try again in a moment.

Never

  • explains how the tech works
  • pushes you to buy or spend
  • jokes when money is at stake
  • says “sats”, “BTC” or “mint”

  • Setup and the lock screen
  • Every payment that comes in
  • Empty states and gentle errors

Not here

  • Never on top of numbers or the recovery words
  • Never more than one Zappy on a screen
